---
title: "Guanyin and Tea Culture"
date: 2026-05-20T10:00:00Z
---

Guanyin is widely known in East Asia as the Bodhisattva of Compassion. Tea houses and tea drinkers often place Guanyin images nearby as a reminder of calmness, patience, and kindness.

In many Fujian tea communities, tea preparation is treated as a mindful practice: warming the teaware, observing aroma, and sharing each infusion with respect. This spirit of care is one reason Guanyin is often linked with tea culture.
