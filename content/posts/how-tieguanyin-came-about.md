---
title: "How Tieguanyin Tea Came About"
date: 2026-05-21T10:00:00Z
---

Tieguanyin (Iron Goddess of Mercy) is a famous oolong tea traditionally associated with Anxi, Fujian.

One popular origin story says a poor farmer faithfully cared for a small Guanyin temple. In gratitude, Guanyin appeared in a dream and guided him to a tea shoot hidden behind a rock. He planted and propagated it, and the tea developed a rich floral fragrance and smooth body.

Whether told through legend or local history, the name Tieguanyin reflects both strength ("iron") and compassion ("Guanyin"). Today, Tieguanyin remains one of the best-known Chinese oolong teas.
